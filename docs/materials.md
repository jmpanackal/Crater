## Three discovery types (LOCKED)

| Type | Is | Handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Materials** | Bulk physical resources — “we have the stuff” | Visibly hauled/tethered behind the player; can be cached locally; abstract quantities once in established storage |
| **Components** | Smaller manufactured/specialized objects, usually ship-derived — “we have the part” | Lightweight personal storage |
| **Records** | Knowledge — “we know how” | Teach, reinterpret, expose contradictions, reveal routes, unlock district projects, Approved capabilities, or Forbidden Designs. Never generic “Knowledge +1”. |

Not every technology requires all three.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Materials should support more than one use; no Material exists only to feed one district. Names like Sporemeal, Threadroot, Lampwick (as a mined Material), Charstone, Deeprock, Lunore, Karn, and Tetzal are cut.

## Deposits and extraction (LOCKED)

- Most broken terrain yields **nothing**. Materials come from recognizable deposits in environmentally logical **resource pockets** (damp fungal cavities, water/mineral pockets, dense impact strata, buried ship-fracture zones, old service cavities, flooded ship spaces).
- Flow: **discover → expose/excavate → extract → haul/store/use**.
- Natural deposits are finite and do not regenerate. District production renews; natural Materials do not.
- Extraction feels modestly different per Material without separate minigames; better equipment makes impractical extraction possible rather than adding yield percentages.
- Depletion uses modular overlays: **base terrain + intact deposit overlay → depleted overlay**.

## Hauling, caching, and ownership (LOCKED)

- Hauling reserves stamina; depositing or caching releases it. Physics prioritizes readability over rope simulation.
- A **cache** is an optional local frontier stash, not a global inventory.
- Returning home does not auto-dump Materials into communal storage. Personal finds in allowed areas are generally the player's; Materials gathered for accepted work, assigned duty, civic excavation, or a promised project carry a delivery expectation.
- Normal Material delivery primarily earns **Tallies**; Trust changes only for socially meaningful behavior.

## District output (LOCKED — see canon §24–§25)

Each district (Glowbeds, Wickwork, Cistern) tracks **Production Capacity, Civic Demand, Reserve, Reserve Cap, and Unmet Demand**, resolved once per civic cycle (around Ritual → Rousing). Output is abstracted into units that fictionally represent real goods — prepared biological products, fabricated goods, pressure/mechanical supplies. There are no canon named goods.

- **Approved Gear Orders** consume Tallies + authorized district output.
- **Diversion** moves output from Reserve into concealed personal storage toward Forbidden Gear. It does not lower Capacity; its harm is the lost buffer.
