# Hollow chunk atlas

The atlas is the implementation-facing, full-Hollow board: [`refs/hollow-grid-compositor.html`](refs/hollow-grid-compositor.html).

It is a **single continuous atlas canvas** with fixed coordinate alignment. Until a shared composition master is approved, each occupied coordinate displays its own full 16:9 reference image at its exact cell bounds; no asset may cover a neighboring cell. Once a master exists, it must have matching cell-sized derivative frames before it is rendered in the atlas. This preserves both visible adjacency and normal-camera chunk IDs for Godot handoff.

## Level 0 topology and image hierarchy

The 18 × 16 board is **Level 0**: the exact, non-AI spatial plan. It is the authority for district placement, progression, routes, and every cell's adjacency. A generated macro image may be displayed beneath it for review, but cannot change its geography.

Visual production proceeds downward from the fixed Level 0 plan:

1. **Level 1 — regional masters:** 12 compositions arranged as 3 columns × 4 rows. Each owns a 6 × 4 Level 0 region and establishes the region's local silhouette, routes, void depth, and palette.
2. **Level 2A — anchor masters:** each Level 1 region splits into 3 × 2 shared 2 × 2-cell compositions. There are 72 anchor masters. An anchor master is generated as one 16:9 image, never as four unrelated neighboring images.
3. **Level 2B — bridge masters:** create an overlapping 2 × 2 master for every walkable or visually important seam that crosses an anchor-master boundary. Its output supplies only a narrow shared seam band to the two adjacent child frames; it never replaces their whole interiors. On the opening route, `H-2-11 ↔ H-3-11` is internal to one anchor; `H-3-11 ↔ H-4-11` is the crossing that needs a bridge master.
4. **Level 3 — cell frames:** crop the four 16:9 child frames directly from an approved anchor master. Those four frames share literal source pixels along their internal borders. When a bridge master is approved, composite its shared seam band into both adjacent frames before revalidating the pair. A cell may receive a later interior-only revision, but its inherited border bands must be composited back exactly and revalidated against its neighbors.

The hierarchy does not make an AI model perfectly obey every boundary. It prevents geography drift by anchoring every generation to a parent composition; anchor masters make their internal seams exact by derivation, while bridge masters make cross-anchor seams exact without replacing approved cell interiors.

## Pass order: topology before visual differentiation

The current atlas pass is a **continuity pass**, not the final art pass. Its approval confirms that a coordinate belongs in the right place, routes and height changes make physical sense, camera-scale framing is plausible, and every visible transition has a controllable shared source. It does **not** mean a frame has final district-specific prop density or visual identity.

During this pass, prioritize in this order:

1. fixed Level 0 geography and progression;
2. literal shared edges inside anchors and bridge-banded edges between anchors;
3. walkable grades, enclosure, and believable civic transitions;
4. only then local atmosphere and props.
5. preserve the established visual grammar—materials, lift dimensions, building language, and lamp treatment—while varying civic function, route shape, silhouette, and spatial arrangement.

### Interior-enclosure rule

Only cells physically adjacent to the Devil’s Mouth may read out into its open depth. Every other wall district—including worker homes, Wickwork, Glowbeds, Mid-East dig access, lower-east housing, and Cistern service—must terminate into thick crater rock, a carved court, a local shaft, a retaining wall, or an inward-facing service bay. A walkable terrace in an interior district needs a visible rock-backed or enclosed far edge; it is never an unexplained balcony above blue abyss. Narrow sightlines through a guarded fracture are permitted, but they cannot replace a physically grounded route.

The west worker connector at `H-4-7`–`H-5-8` is the reference repair for this rule: one shared 2 × 2 master gives it an enclosed diagonal switchback, local hand freight, and a controlled entry toward Mid Heart. It has no operating lower-west lift and no open pit balcony.

The lower continuation at `H-4`–`H-5`, rows `9`–`10`, follows the same rule: it is a shared, rock-backed worker/home approach with a short stair and a sealed eastern service side. `H-4-11` remains the immutable Home Court anchor; `H-5-11` is a contained service turnback, never a lift landing or a second exit.

### Landmark-first spatial rule

Do not generate the Hollow as stacked horizontal room bands. Before making a new anchor, assign it one **multi-cell landmark** that crosses at least one cell boundary: a switchback, steep/short stair run, tall carved bay, local hydraulic service lift, hanging freight gantry, recessed court, or open public terrace. The anchor’s rooms and props must conform to that landmark rather than creating clean parallel floors.

At least one of each region’s major spaces must be an **open terrace** large enough for its civic function, with visible railings/cables/supports and a view into Devil’s Mouth only where that geography is truly adjacent, otherwise a large interior shaft or enclosed court. Pair it with tighter caves, deeper carved rooms, and overhangs so the settlement reads as an uneven inhabited cliffscape. At outer-wall limits, transition into thick rock, collapse, braced gates, sealed galleries, and maintained turnbacks—never another generic room. Seam masters must continue the landmark’s route and silhouette, not merely erase a black gap.

### 2D gameplay readability rule

Every atlas cell must read as a side-scroller play space before it reads as concept art: one dominant collision floor, at most one clearly railed secondary ledge, and explicit stair/ramp landings. In a shared 2 × 2 master, use no more than three traversable elevations. Pipes, distant buildings, rigging, tank faces, and deep-Mouth silhouettes are background or blockers rather than ambiguous walkways. The rebuilt `H-8`–`H-9`, rows `6`–`7` Holding plate is the current Mid Heart reference: one public upper route, one short local descent, and a deliberate lower void cut.

Every shared image must be normalized to its intended tile canvas before cropping: a 2 × 2 parent is exactly 2048 × 1152 before its four 1024 × 576 children are exported. This is map integrity, not just export hygiene: it makes routes, rock volumes, and void cuts literal across the internal seams. The rebuilt `H-6`–`H-7`, rows `6`–`7` West Exchange approach is the complementary reference: it is rock-backed except for one narrow, railed gangway into the Mouth-facing Holding edge.

### Locked transport topology

The atlas must show the network as three distinct civic machines, not two mirrored all-height shafts. The **west civic lift** starts in the Wickwork/Mid band and rises toward High-West and the west Ashram gateway; lower-west cells therefore use walkable worker switchbacks and ramps to approach Mid Heart. The **east Cistern freight lift** is the heavy Low-to-Mid system between deep Cistern, lower-east service, and Mid-East. The **east upper passenger lift** is separate: it begins at a recessed guarded Mid-East landing and rises through Glowbeds toward the east Ashram gateway. It must sit away from the Devil’s Mouth edge. A cell image may show shaft, cage, landing, or switchback, but never imply a lift continues beyond its locked route.

After the continuous map is complete, run a non-destructive **visual-differentiation pass**. It will revise interiors while preserving approved edge bands and topology: homes become domestic, Dispatch becomes logistics-led, dig faces become raw/brace-led, return routes become service-led, and civic thresholds become ordered rather than generic workshop scenery. Do not solve repetition by breaking a previously approved seam.

## Operating rules

1. Every planned chunk has a fixed slot before its art is made.
2. Each occupied coordinate visibly owns one full image frame. A draft is clearly amber-marked and is never recorded as an approved implementation image. A Level 2 shared seam master supplies its four derived frames; it may not obscure a neighboring coordinate's frame in the board.
3. Do not replace an approved reference destructively. Add a dated sibling image, review it, then update that slot's `image` field and retain the old file as history.
4. Every slot must record its route seams, camera contract, systemic owner, and level band. A beautiful image is not approved for implementation until its adjacent seams share boundary height/grade, route/rail continuation, and camera overlap. Neighboring reference art is generated as a Level 2 shared 2 × 2 seam master; normal-camera crops are derived for implementation and rendered at their exact slots.
5. Atlas `x`, `y`, `w`, and `h` positions are **design topology coordinates**, not final Godot world pixels. The Godot implementation must translate them into scene-local chunk origins, tile/collision extents, and camera bounds while preserving the stated adjacencies.

### Default review placement

Every newly generated anchor is immediately normalized, cropped into its exact Level 0 cells, and rendered in the atlas as **amber draft frames**. This makes the growing composition visible before a review response. A rejection removes those draft references without deleting their source history; approval promotes the same parent and cell-frame lineage to approved status. Bridge masters remain non-destructive seam sources until applied to their affected descendants.

## Implementation handoff

When constructing a chunk in Godot, copy the slot's ID into the scene/region naming convention and record:

- scene or region path;
- world origin and walkable bounds;
- east/west/north/south seam transforms and destination IDs;
- camera anchor / clamp range;
- relevant district, production, home, lift, Warden, Work Order, Trust, or theft hooks;
- the currently approved image filename and date.

This keeps visual approval, traversal construction, and story/system integration synchronized.
